1Information We Process and How We Use It

Our App is designed with a privacy-first, on-device architecture. This means we minimize the collection of personal data and prioritize local processing.

A. Camera Data & Real-Time Scanning (On-Device Processing)

  • What we process: the App requires access to your device's camera to detect, capture, and decode QR codes and barcodes.
  • How we handle it: visual data from your camera stream is processed strictly in real-time, in-memory, and locally on your device.
We do not record, save, store, or transmit your camera feed or images to any external servers.

B. Scan History & Local Storage

  • What we process: text contents, barcode formats, custom tags, and timestamps of your scanned items.
  • How we handle it: scan history and related app data are stored locally on the device where they were created. We do not operate a cloud database for your scan history, and this history is not automatically synchronized or transferred between your devices. If you change devices, reinstall the App, or clear App data, locally stored history may no longer be available unless you previously exported or otherwise backed it up. Where available, you can use the App’s export features to save a copy of your history.

C. Google Play Subscriptions & Payment Information

  • What we process: the App checks Google Play for the status of Pro subscription products scanner_pro_monthly and scanner_pro_yearly so that eligible Pro features can be enabled.
  • How we handle it: purchases, renewals, cancellations, and payment credentials are handled directly by Google Play Billing. Blue Acid Labs does not collect or store your credit card number, bank account details, or Google Play payment credentials.
  • Changing devices: if you install the App on another compatible device using the same Google account that owns an active subscription, Google Play may make that subscription available for the App to restore or recognize. Restoring Pro status does not transfer your locally stored scan history, tags, or other device-only data.

D. Local Settings & Device Data

  • What we process: App preferences such as your selected language, settings, and other device-only state needed for App features.
  • How we handle it: the App does not require a Blue Acid Labs account, sign-in, or registration. These settings are stored locally on your device and are not used as proof of a Pro subscription. Pro entitlement is checked through Google Play.

2Device Permissions Required

To deliver core scanning services, the App may request the following system permissions:

  • Camera Permission: required to capture and decode real-time barcodes. You may revoke this permission at any time in your device's operating system settings. If revoked, you can still scan barcode images by uploading them manually.
  • Storage / Photos Access (Optional): required only if you choose to scan a QR code from a pre-existing image file saved on your device, or when exporting your scan history to a local CSV or JSON file.

3Third-Party Services & Advertisements

The free version of the App uses Google services for advertising, consent, and Play functionality. These services may process technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies and your applicable consent choices:

  • Google Play / Google Play Billing: provides App distribution, subscription purchasing, restoration, and subscription-status services.
  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): the free version may display ads. Google may process device or advertising identifiers, consent choices, and related technical information to provide and measure ads, subject to applicable law and your consent choices. Pro users do not receive in-App ads from this implementation.

4Data Sharing and Disclosure

  • No Data Sales: we do not sell, trade, rent, or monetize your personal information or scanned contents to third parties under any circumstances.
  • No Third-Party Sharing of Scans: the content of your scanned QR codes and barcodes is processed entirely locally and is never shared with third parties, unless you explicitly trigger a native share action to send the scanned text to another app of your choice.

5Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the App’s local-first design. Because scan history and related records are stored on your device, protecting that device and any exported files is important. Device loss, App removal, clearing App data, or moving to a new device can result in loss of locally stored history because the App does not provide cloud synchronization of scan history.

6Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children. If we discover that any anonymous telemetry from a child under 13 has been processed, we will take immediate steps to remove it.

7Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect functional adjustments to the App or changes in privacy regulations. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Effective Date."
